# The reported artifact cameras, one per geometry fault the player has found.
# Their job is not the image -- it is the counters printed with it: a facet
# nobody designed (`edgelong`, `tritiny`), a point nobody used (`unusedpts`), a
# seabed colour on a facet that has a dry corner (`wetcoldry`), a seam chord
# standing off the ground (`seamgap`). Each must reach zero as PLAN.md's
# P1..P4 land; today they are alarms set at the measured worst.
#
#   ./build/game replay replays/artifacts.txt --seed 1 --world alpine --size 1280x720
#
# The images land in build/shots/art-*.png next to their .small.png, so a fix
# can be looked at as well as counted.

# structural health after 6 views (which stream in every tier at least once)
assert edgelong <= 20
assert tritiny <= 120
assert wetcoldry <= 80
assert seamgap <= 200
# How far a facet's own plane is from the ground under it, in thirds of a tier
# spacing (1.0 = a third). At step 1 it is a bug -- the shard; at tier 4 it is a
# cliff, which no insertion into the plane can fix, so it has its own alarm.
assert grounderr <= 12
assert groundfar <= 16
# pool exhaustion and stripped uniforms are warnings: they must never appear
assert warned == 0
assert chunkverts < 88
# A point the fill placed and the triangulation then ignored: the weld repairs
# both cases (inside a facet, and on an edge of one), so this is an invariant now.
assert unusedpts == 0
# facets re-lit by the ground's normal instead of their own plane: the
# prevalence of the dark-chord mechanism, not a defect count. It is 49-59 here;
# a jump means the fill is producing chords faster than the fill fixes them.
assert relit <= 90
# Hull lids dropped: a triangle with all three corners on the border ring is the
# triangle the convex hull leaves when the jittered chain bulges inward, and where
# its three corners' heights span a cliff it is a vertical sheet standing face-on
# to a camera across the valley -- the rock wedge at the coast. Cumulative over
# every chunk rebuilt in the replay, hence the size; the point of the assert is
# that the pass is alive, so a real change shows up as a decision here.
assert lid > 100
# Foam belongs on a channel. The fall flag is decided per *point* so that a
# cascade is continuous (per facet it broke into dashes, because one facet is
# one facet wide on a steep reach), and the facet still has to be on the channel
# or whole rock faces under a summit stream turn white. This count is 0.
assert foamfar == 0
# An invariant, not an alarm: a facet that the builder called water must emit a
# water surface. This is 0; the day it is not, the lake has a hole in it and the
# player sees the far cliff through it.
assert nowater == 0
