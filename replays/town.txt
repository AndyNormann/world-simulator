# M2: one place. The town the sim placed at site 3 of seed 12, seen and walked.
#
#   ./build/game dump replays/town.txt --seed 12 --settlement 3   asserts only
#   tools/render.sh replay replays/town.txt --seed 12 --settlement 3
#
# The coordinates are the generated layout: the street goes between the row at
# z=829.5 and the row at z=854.5, and the first house there is at x=3890. If a
# change moves the houses, this fails loudly -- which is the point, because a
# town that silently relocates is a bug in the placement, not in the test.
#
# Re-recorded when the water became its own geometry: the beds under lakes and
# rivers dropped a few units, fertility moved with them, and the site the sim
# picks for seed 12 moved 274 units west (centre 4262,962 -> 3988,962) and grew
# from 80 buildings to 119. Not a placement bug: the ground really is better
# there now.

# walk north out of the street into a house: a wall stops you, and the walk is
# not a teleport (30 u/s for 2 s would cover 60 units)

assert cam_z > 835
assert cam_z < 842

# the same walk with no town around it is not blocked

look 180 -5

assert cam_z < 2390
